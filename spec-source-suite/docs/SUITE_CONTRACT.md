# Shared rules

1. The selected [Markdown bundle](MARKDOWN_BUNDLES.md) and its normative references define required behavior. Plans and memory cannot override them. Preserve explicitly approved text and existing history. A direct implementation request authorizes derived planning and ordinary implementation choices; ask only about conflicting requirements or decisions outside that authority.
2. Save the complete plan and source-linked acceptance before implementation. Use [Define](DEFINE.md) for preparation and document-only completion. Keep app artifacts and evidence outside the suite.
3. Implement within the selected scope. Run proportionate checks of observable requirements, relevant regressions, and required real-dependency/manual checks. Reuse useful tests; add coverage for gaps through public interfaces. Avoid assertions tied to internals, mocks that supply the result being asserted, and production hooks added only for tests.
4. Use real dependencies where practical. Record substitute limits or impractical checks with their remaining evidence and coverage gaps; resolve ordinary testing choices autonomously. Required unavailable capabilities block acceptance. A skipped check is NOT_RUN or BLOCKED, never PASS; an observed requirement violation remains FAIL. Do not weaken requirements or expectations to make a failure pass.
5. Each candidate needs a fresh reviewer without inherited conversation. Supply requirements, acceptance, implementation, checks/results, relevant dependency contracts and prior findings; exclude plans, conversations and project memory. The reviewer independently inspects and executes relevant checks. Missing fresh-review capability is INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_NOT_RUN and blocks acceptance.
6. Keep the implementer through repairs. After changes, rerun affected checks/regressions and spawn a new fresh reviewer until no actionable findings remain and evidence supports every requirement. Stop only for cancellation, applicable limits, conflicting requirements or unavailable capabilities; record unfinished work. Preserve findings and their resolutions without exhaustive attempt inventories.
7. A task unlocks dependents only after acceptance. Changed requirements, implementations or dependencies require reassessing affected results and rerunning relevant checks/reviews. Generate must execute final scenarios on the assembled app before claiming completion; individual task passes are insufficient.
8. Record concise [results](../templates/RUN_REPORT.md): scope/candidate, requirement-linked checks and outcomes, review findings/resolutions, blockers, evidence and limitations. Distinguish PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED and NOT_RUN, execution from coverage judgment, and manual observations from automation. Include [usage statistics](HELPERS.md#usage-reporting), even on blocked runs; missing telemetry never blocks app acceptance.
9. Ordinary planners and implementers read relevant [memory](PROJECT_MEMORY.md) and save useful lessons at completion or a blocked stop. Reviewers and [reconstruction workers](REGENERATION.md) exclude memory. Report actual isolation limits; instructions alone do not enforce isolation.
